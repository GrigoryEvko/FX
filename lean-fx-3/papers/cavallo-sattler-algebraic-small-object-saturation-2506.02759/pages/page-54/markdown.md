**Example 4.3.22.** Shulman [Shu19, §3] introduces the framework of *notions of fibred structure* to axiomatize algebraic incarnations of classes of maps such as fibrations and trivial fibrations that are closed under base change. In particular, the categories R-Alg and $\mathsf{R}_p$-Alg associated to an AWFS (L, R) on a category $\mathcal{E}$ with pullbacks give rise to notions of fibred structure in $\mathcal{E}$ [Shu19, Example 3.7] [BG16a, Proposition 8].

Given a notion of fibred structure on a category $\mathcal{E}$, we have in particular a Grothendieck fibration $P: \mathcal{F} \to \mathcal{E}$ from a category of structured maps $\mathcal{F}$ that projects the codomain of a structured map. Shulman's Proposition 3.18(i)$\Rightarrow$(iii) implies that for a *locally representable* notion of fibred structure on $\mathcal{E}$, any colimit in $\mathcal{E}$ that is Van Kampen for the codomain fibration cod: $\mathcal{E}^- \to \mathcal{E}$ is also Van Kampen for the induced fibration $P$. Provided that $\mathcal{E}$ has enough Van Kampen colimits, extension operations for such structures are thus susceptible to Theorem 4.3.19. Indeed, Example 4.3.21 could be factored through a proof that the notion of fibred structure corresponding to uniform fibration monad algebras is locally representable.

## 5 Related work and unanswered questions

### 5.1 Coalgebraic cell complexes

Our work complements that of Athorne on *coalgebraic cell complexes* [Ath12; Ath14]. Athorne shows that under various conditions [Ath14, §3.3] on the category $\mathcal{E}$ and generating diagram $u: \mathcal{J} \to \mathcal{E}^-$, the double category L-Coalg of comonad coalgebras for the AWFS cofibrantly generated by $u$ is equivalent to a double category of formal cell complexes [Ath14, Theorem 3.5.1]. Compared to Athorne's results, ours are weaker in that we do not give any *characterization* of the double category of coalgebras. Our Theorem 3.5.16 produces a pseudo double functor from $\mathsf{L}_p$-Coalg into some target, but it does not show that this functor is in some sense uniquely determined, so cannot be used as a universal property to derive an equivalence.

For our less ambitious goal of extending various structures from generators to left maps, our theorems offer a simpler interface, require checking fewer conditions, and are more general. In particular, our results apply to *any* AWFS cofibrantly generated by a diagram that satisfies our smallness conditions in a cocomplete category, though their usefulness depends on the AWFS and choice of backdrop.

We note that our backdrops play a role analogous to that of Athorne's class of *typical inclusions* [Ath12, Definition 3.3.1] (which is induced by a choice of *typical nerve* [Ath12, Definition 3.3.2]). Whereas Athorne requires the typical inclusions to be monomorphisms, the conditions on a backdrop are always satisfied by the class of all morphisms of a category with the necessary colimits; that said, our concrete applications all use backdrops of monomorphisms.
