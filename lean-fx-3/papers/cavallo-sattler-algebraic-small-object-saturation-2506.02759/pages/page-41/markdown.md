**Definition 4.1.1.** A *uniform fibration configuration* $(I, t)$ in a presheaf category $\mathcal{E} = \mathrm{PSh}(\mathcal{C})$ consists of

(a) a *cofibration classifier*: a monomorphism $t: 1 \mapsto \mathbb{F}$;

(b) a *functorial cylinder*: a functor $I: \mathcal{C} \to \mathcal{C}$ with monomorphism-valued natural transformations $\delta^0, \delta^1: \mathrm{Id}_{\mathcal{C}} \to I$ and a Yoneda extension $I_!: \mathcal{E} \to \mathcal{E}$ such that

(i) $I_!$ preserves pullbacks;

(ii) the induced natural transformations $\delta^0, \delta^1: \mathrm{Id}_{\mathcal{E}} \to I_!$ are cartesian.

We write $I^*$ for the right adjoint to $I_!$ and $\partial_i: I^* \to \mathrm{Id}_{\mathcal{E}}$ for the conjugate of $\delta^i: \mathrm{Id}_{\mathcal{E}} \to I_!$.

**Definition 4.1.2.** Given $f: Y \to X$ in $\mathcal{E} = \mathrm{PSh}(\mathcal{C})$, write $\phi^f: \mathcal{E}/X \to \mathcal{E}^\to$ for the functor that sends an object $a: B \to X$ to the base change

$$\begin{array}{c} A \xrightarrow{\quad} Y \\ \phi^f(a) \Big\downarrow \quad \Big\downarrow f \\ B \xrightarrow{a} X. \end{array}$$

Write $u^f: \int_{\mathcal{C}} X \to \mathcal{E}^\to$ for the composite $\int_{\mathcal{C}} X \xrightarrow{\mathfrak{k}} \mathcal{E}/X \xrightarrow{\phi^f} \mathcal{E}^\to$.

**Definition 4.1.3.** Fix $\mathcal{E} = \mathrm{PSh}(\mathcal{C})$ with a uniform fibration configuration $(t, I)$ as in Definition 4.1.1. Write $\mathsf{box}_I^t := [\widehat{\delta^0} u^t, \widehat{\delta^1} u^t]$ for the diagram

$$\begin{array}{c} \int_{\mathcal{C}} \mathbb{F} \xrightarrow{\upsilon_0} (\int_{\mathcal{C}} \mathbb{F}) \sqcup (\int_{\mathcal{C}} \mathbb{F}) \xleftarrow{\upsilon_1} \int_{\mathcal{C}} \mathbb{F}. \\ \Bigg\downarrow \quad \Bigg\downarrow \quad \Bigg\downarrow \quad \mathsf{box}_I^t \\ \mathcal{E}^\to \quad \mathcal{E}^\to \quad \mathcal{E}^\to \quad \mathcal{E}^\to \end{array}$$

The *uniform fibration* AWFS on $(t, I)$, when it exists, is the AWFS cofibrantly generated by $\mathsf{box}_I^t$. We call the left maps of the AWFS *trivial cofibrations*. We write $\mathrm{TCof_p}(t, I)$ and $\mathrm{TCof_p}(t, I)$ for the category and double category of copointed endofunctor coalgebras respectively.

For a monomorphism $m: A \mapsto B$, we think of $\widehat{\delta^i}(m)$ as an “generalized open box”: its codomain is the $B$-shaped cylinder $I_!B$, and its domain, the pushout $B \sqcup_A I_!A$, comprises one end of the cylinder (determined by $i \in \{0, 1\}$) together with the $A$-shaped sub-part of its interior and other end. Such open boxes have long been used for example in simplicial [GZ67, §IV.2] and cubical [Cis06, §8.4] homotopy theory.

Classically, the uniform fibration AWFS exists for any choice of configuration, as presheaf categories are locally presentable. Constructively, even without quotients in **Set**, we can obtain it via Theorem 3.2.12 under additional finiteness and decidability assumptions on $(t, I)$.

**Definition 4.1.4.** A map $f: Y \to X$ in $\mathcal{E} = \mathrm{PSh}(\mathcal{C})$ is *locally* $(\kappa, \mathcal{M})$-*small* if for every $a \in \int_{\mathcal{C}} X$ the domain of $u^f(a)$ is $(\kappa, \mathcal{M})$-small. We say $f$ is *locally finite* if for every $a \in \int_{\mathcal{C}} X$, the domain of $u^f(a)$ is a finite colimit of representables.

A locally finite map is locally $(\kappa, \mathcal{M})$-small for all $\kappa$ and $\mathcal{M}$, since representables are $(\kappa, \mathcal{M})$-small and the $(\kappa, \mathcal{M})$-small objects are closed under finite colimits.

**Definition 4.1.5.** A uniform fibration configuration $(t, I)$ is *finitary* when

(a) $t: 1 \mapsto \mathbb{F}$ is levelwise complemented and locally finite.
