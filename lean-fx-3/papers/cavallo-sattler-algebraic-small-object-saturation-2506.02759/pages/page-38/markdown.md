Definition 3.5.10. For a notion of composable structure \( U \colon \mathbb{A} \to \mathrm{Sq}(\mathcal{E}) \), a codomain retract lifting operator is a functor \( \mathbb{A}^{\sharp} \times_{\mathcal{E}^{-}} \operatorname{CodRet}(\mathcal{E}) \to \mathbb{A}^{\sharp} \), where the domain is the pullback of \( U^{\sharp} \colon \mathbb{A}^{\sharp} \to \mathcal{E}^{-} \) and \( e_1 \colon \operatorname{CodRet}(\mathcal{E}) \to \mathcal{E}^{-} \), fitting into the diagram

\[
\begin{array}{c} \mathbb {A} ^ {\sharp} \times_ {\mathcal {E} ^ {-}} \operatorname{CodRet} (\mathcal {E}) \xrightarrow {} \mathbb {A} ^ {\sharp} \\ \pi_ {1} \Big \downarrow \qquad \qquad \qquad \qquad \qquad \qquad \qquad \qquad \qquad \Big \downarrow U \\ \operatorname{CodRet} (\mathcal {E}) \xrightarrow [ e _ {0} ]{} \mathcal {E} ^ {-}. \end{array}
\]

Example 3.5.11. Given an AWFS \((\mathsf{L},\mathsf{R})\) on a category \(\mathcal{E}\), \(U_{\mathsf{L}_{\mathfrak{p}}}\colon \mathsf{L}_{\mathfrak{p}}\text{-Coalg} \to \mathbb{S}\mathfrak{q}(\mathcal{E})\) admits a codomain retract lifting operator.

Proof. Recall that a vertical morphism over \( f \in \mathcal{E}^{-} \) in \( \mathsf{L}_{\mathfrak{p}} \)-Coalg is a section \( \sigma \colon f \to Lf \) of \( \Phi_f \). Given a retract \( g \xrightarrow{\alpha} f \xrightarrow{\beta} g \) in \( \mathcal{E}^{-} \), it follows from naturality of \( \Phi \) that \( L\beta \circ \sigma \circ \alpha \colon g \to Lg \) is a section of \( \Phi_g \), and this assignment is evidently functorial.

Remark 3.5.12. Note that a codomain retract lifting operator only lifts the retract object, not entire retract diagrams. In Example 3.5.11, for instance, we cannot expect that \((\alpha, \beta)\) exhibits \(L\beta \circ \sigma \circ \alpha: g \to Lg\) as a retract of \(\sigma: f \to Lf\) in \(\mathsf{L}_{\mathfrak{p}}\)-Coalg.

Theorem 3.5.13. Fix a category \(\mathcal{E}\) equipped with a \(\kappa\)-backdrop \(\mathcal{M}\), a diagram \(u: \mathcal{J} \to \mathcal{E}^{-}\) compatible with \(\mathcal{M}\) such that \(\operatorname{dom} u\) is levelwise \((\kappa, \mathcal{M})\)-small, and a left-connected, \((\kappa, \mathcal{M})\)-cellular notion of composable structure \(U: \mathbb{A} \to \operatorname{Sq}(\mathcal{E})\) with a codomain retract lifting operator. If \((\mathsf{L}, \mathsf{R})\) is the AWFS cofibrantly generated by \(u\), then any lift \(D_{\mathbb{A}}: \mathcal{E}^{-} \to \mathbb{A}^{\sharp}\) of \(D_u: \mathcal{E}^{-} \to \mathcal{E}^{-}\) through \(U^{\sharp}\) induces a functor \(j: \mathsf{L}_{\mathfrak{p}}\)-Coalg \(\to \mathbb{A}^{\sharp}\) fitting in the diagram

\[
\begin{array}{c} \mathsf {L} _ {\mathrm{p}} \text {-Coalg} \xrightarrow {j} \mathsf {A} ^ {\sharp}. \\ U _ {\mathsf {L} _ {\mathrm{p}}} \searrow_ {\mathcal {E} ^ {-}} \swarrow U ^ {\sharp} \end{array}
\]

Proof. Under the assumptions above, we can apply Theorem 3.5.6 to obtain lifts \( \pmb{L}_{\mathbb{A}}\colon \mathcal{E}^{-}\to \mathbb{A}^{\sharp} \) of \( L\colon \mathcal{E}^{-}\to \mathcal{E}^{-} \) through \( U^{\sharp} \) such that step\(_u\colon D_u\to L\) lifts to a transformation \( D_{\mathbb{A}}\to L_{\mathbb{A}} \). For any object \( \pmb {f} = (f,s)\in \mathsf{L}_{\mathrm{p}}\text{-Coalg} \), we have by definition codomain retract diagram

\[
f \xrightarrow {(\mathrm{id} , s)} L f \xrightarrow {\Phi_ {f}} f \tag {3.5}
\]

By assumption, we can lift  \( L_{A}f \in A \)  along this retract to obtain  \( jf \in A^{\sharp} \)  with  \( U^{\sharp}(jf) = f \) . Similarly, any morphism  \( (h, k) \) :  \( f \to g \)  in  \( L_{p} \) -Coalg induces by definition a morphism of retract diagrams as above and a morphism  \( \mathbf{L}_{\mathbb{A}}(h, k) \) :  \( L_{A}f \to L_{A}g \) , whence a choice of lift  \( j(h, k) \) :  \( jf \to jg \)  with  \( U(j(h, k)) = (h, k) \) .

To get a (pseudo) double functor from  \( L_{p} \) -Coalg, we require an additional compositionality condition on the retract lifting operator. For the sake of simplicity, we now only consider the case where A is thin.

Definition 3.5.14. A codomain retract lifting operator on a thin notion of composable structure \( U \colon \mathbb{A} \to \mathrm{Sq}(\mathcal{E}) \) is compositional if for all diagrams

\[
\begin{array}{c c} B ^ {\prime} \xrightarrow {s _ {B}} B \xrightarrow {r _ {B}} B ^ {\prime} & \\ B ^ {\prime} \xrightarrow {s _ {B}} B \xrightarrow {r _ {B}} B ^ {\prime} & \\ \downarrow g ^ {\prime} \quad \downarrow g \quad g ^ {\prime} \downarrow & \\ C ^ {\prime} \xrightarrow {u} C \xrightarrow {v} C ^ {\prime} & \\ C ^ {\prime \prime} \xrightarrow {s _ {C}} C ^ {\prime} \xrightarrow {r _ {C}} C ^ {\prime \prime} & \end{array} \tag {3.6}
\]

such that  \( r_{C}vu = r_{C} \) , the chosen lift of  \( g \star f \)  along the composite codomain retract  \( g''f' \to gf \to g''f' \)  is isomorphic over  \( g''f' \)  to the vertical composite of the chosen lift of  \( g' \)  along  \( g'' \to g' \to g'' \)  with the chosen lift of f along  \( f' \to f \to f' \) .
