**Lemma 4.3.18.** Let $P \colon \mathcal{E} \to \mathcal{B}$ be a Grothendieck fibration and $d \colon \mathcal{K} \to \mathcal{B}^-$ be a diagram. If $\delta \colon d \to \Delta d_0$ is a colimit cocone such that $\operatorname{dom} \circ \delta$ and $\operatorname{cod} \circ \delta$ are Van Kampen for $P$, then $\delta$ is Van Kampen for $P^{\rightarrow \rightarrow} \colon \mathcal{E}^{\rightarrow \rightarrow} \to \mathcal{B}^-$.

*Proof.* Since colimits in arrow categories are computed pointwise, it suffices to check that for any diagram $e \colon \mathcal{K} \to \mathcal{E}^{\rightarrow \rightarrow}$ over $d$, the induced morphism $\operatorname{colim}(\operatorname{dom} \circ e) \to \operatorname{colim}(\operatorname{cod} \circ e)$ in $\mathcal{E}$ is $P$-cartesian. This is the case because $\operatorname{colim}(\operatorname{dom} \circ e)$ is a colimit in $\mathcal{E}_{\mathcal{B}\text{-cart}}$, because $\operatorname{dom} \circ \delta$ is Van Kampen for $P$. $\square$

By the saturation theorem, we conclude:

**Theorem 4.3.19.** Let $\mathcal{E}$ be a category with a $\kappa$-backdrop $\mathcal{M}$ and a diagram $u \colon \mathcal{J} \to \mathcal{E}^-$ compatible with $\mathcal{M}$ such that $\operatorname{dom} u$ is levelwise $(\kappa, \mathcal{M})$-small. Write $(\mathsf{L}, \mathsf{R})$ for the AWFS cofibrantly generated by $u$. Let $P \colon \mathcal{F} \to \mathcal{E}$ be a Grothendieck fibration such that

- (a) colimits of $(1 + \alpha)$-chains in $\mathcal{M}$ are Van Kampen for $P$ for $\alpha \preceq \kappa$;
- (b) cobase changes of maps in $\mathcal{M}$ are Van Kampen for $P$.

Any lift of $\mathsf{D}_u \colon \mathcal{E}^- \to \mathcal{E}^-$ through $U_P \colon \operatorname{Ext}_P \to \mathcal{E}^-$ induces a functor $\mathsf{L}_p\text{-Coalg} \to \operatorname{Ext}_P$ over $\mathcal{E}^-$ assigning an extension operation to every left map of the AWFS.

*Proof.* By Theorem 3.5.13. Proposition 4.3.15 gives left-connectedness and Proposition 4.3.16 provides a codomain retract lifting operator. For colimits, Lemmas 4.3.17 and 4.3.18 imply that colimits of $(1 + \alpha)$-chains in $\mathcal{E}^-(\cong \mathcal{M})$ for $\alpha \preceq \kappa$ and cobase changes of maps in $\mathcal{E}^-(\cong \mathcal{M})$ are Van Kampen for $P^{\rightarrow \rightarrow} \colon F^{\rightarrow \rightarrow} \to \mathcal{E}^-$ and $\pi_0 \colon \mathcal{E}^- \times_{\mathcal{E}} \mathcal{F} \to \mathcal{E}^-$. Thus Lemma 4.3.10 implies that $\operatorname{Ext}_P^\downarrow(\cong \mathcal{M})$ is $(\kappa, \mathcal{M})$-cellular. $\square$

Finally, we return to the motivating example of extension of fibrations along trivial cofibrations. When $(\mathsf{L}, \mathsf{R})$ is an AWFS on a category $\mathcal{E}$ with pullbacks, the composite $\operatorname{cod} U_{\mathsf{R}} \colon \mathsf{R}\text{-Alg} \to \mathcal{E}$ is a Grothendieck fibration [BG16a, Proposition 8]. When the AWFS is generated by a diagram of maps with tiny codomain, this fibration inherits the Van Kampen colimits of the underlying category:

**Lemma 4.3.20.** Let $u \colon \mathcal{J} \to \mathcal{E}^-$ be a diagram of arrows in a category with pullbacks and let $(\mathsf{L}, \mathsf{R})$ be an AWFS on $\mathcal{E}$ cofibrantly generated by $u$. Suppose that $\operatorname{cod} u$ is levelwise tiny, in the sense that $\mathcal{E}(\operatorname{cod} u_i, -)$ preserves all colimits for all $i \in \mathcal{J}$. Given a diagram $Y \colon \mathcal{K} \to \mathcal{E}$ and colimit cocone $v \colon Y \to \Delta Y_0$, if $v$ is Van Kampen for $\operatorname{cod} \colon \mathcal{E}^- \to \mathcal{E}$, then $v$ is also Van Kampen for $\operatorname{cod} U_{\mathsf{R}} \colon \mathsf{R}\text{-Alg} \to \mathcal{E}$.

*Proof.* By definition of cofibrantly generated AWFS, an $\mathsf{R}$-algebra structure on $f \in \mathcal{E}^-$ can be described as a section of a map

$$\mathcal{E}(\operatorname{cod} u_{(-)}, \operatorname{dom} f) \xrightarrow{\widetilde{u}_f(f)} \mathcal{E}^-(u_{(-)}, f)$$

that at stage $i \in \mathcal{J}$ sends a solution to a lifting problem $u_i \to f$ to the underlying lifting problem; see the proof of [BG16a, Proposition 16].

Let $\boldsymbol{f} \colon \mathcal{K} \to (\mathsf{R}\text{-Alg})_{\mathcal{E}\text{-cart}}$ be a diagram over $Y$. Then the composite $f := U_{\mathsf{R}}\boldsymbol{f} \colon \mathcal{K} \to \mathcal{E}^-$ factors through $\mathcal{E}_{\text{cart}}^-$ so by assumption admits a colimiting cone $\phi \colon f \to f_0$ in $\mathcal{E}_{\text{cart}}^-$ over $v$. Since $\operatorname{cod} u$ is levelwise tiny, $\mathcal{E}(\operatorname{cod} u_i, v)$ and $\mathcal{E}(\operatorname{cod} u_i, \operatorname{dom} \phi)$ are colimit cocones in **Set**. Because $f$ is a cartesian natural transformation, we have $\mathcal{E}^-(u_i, f_k) \cong \mathcal{E}(\operatorname{cod} u_i, Y_k) \times_{\mathcal{E}(\operatorname{cod} u_i, Y_0)} \mathcal{E}^-(u_i, f_0)$, and so $\mathcal{E}^-(u_i, \phi)$ is also a colimit cocone by pullback stability of colimits in **Set**. Thus the compatible family of sections for the maps $\widetilde{u}_f(f_k)$ provided by $\boldsymbol{f}$ induces a unique compatible section of $\widetilde{u}_f(f_0)$, providing a colimiting cocone $\boldsymbol{f} \to \Delta \boldsymbol{f}_0$ in $(\mathsf{R}\text{-Alg})_{\mathcal{E}\text{-cart}}$ over $v$. $\square$
