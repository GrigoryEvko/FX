Proposition 5.2.1.19. The factorisation of \( p: C^b \to D \) in an initial morphism followed by a left cartesian fibration is

\[
C ^ {b} \xrightarrow {i} D _ {C ^ {b} /} \xrightarrow {q} D,
\]

\[
C ^ {b} \xrightarrow {i} D _ {/ C ^ {b}} \xrightarrow {q} D.
